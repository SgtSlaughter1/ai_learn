This explains what personal data AceDrill keeps, why, who sees it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Contact us, or our Data Protection Officer, at {email}.

## 1. Who is responsible

For learners and staff using AceDrill through a school, **the school is the data controller** and AceDrill processes the data on the school's instructions. For people who sign up on their own, AceDrill is the controller.

## 2. What we keep and why

| Data | Whose | Why we need it | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email, role, school, class, grade level, programme | All users | To run your account and show the right content | While the account exists |
| Password, stored as a one-way hash (nobody can read it) | All users | To sign you in | While the account exists |
| Quiz answers, scores, topic mastery, review schedule, XP, streaks, badges | Learners | To pick your daily review and show progress to you and your teachers | While the account exists |
| School exam attempts: answers, score, time used, how often the exam screen was left | Learners | So the school can run and mark its exams | As long as the school keeps the exam |
| AI tutor chats | All users | So you can read an answer again | Until you delete it, or 1 year after its last message |
| Questions, exams and classes a school creates | Schools | To provide the service | Until the school deletes them or leaves |
| Your consent answers, with the version and date | All users | To show what you agreed to | While the account exists |
| Feedback you send us | Anyone | To fix problems | 2 years |
| Sign-in and security records (session cookies, IP address in short-lived logs) | All users | To keep accounts secure and stop abuse | Up to 90 days |
| AI usage totals per day and feature | No one person | To manage AI costs | Kept as totals, with no names |

We do not sell personal data and do not show advertising.

## 3. Children

Most learners are under 18. We collect a learner's data only when their school confirms it has their parent's or guardian's consent. We collect only what learning needs. We tell the AI tutor a learner's first name and weakest topics, never their full name, email or school.

## 4. Who we share data with

| Who | What they get | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The learner's school and teachers | Progress, scores, exam results | To teach and support the learner |
| AI providers: Groq, Inc. (USA) and Google (Gemini API) | The text of a question or chat message; for the tutor, the learner's first name and weakest topics; for the teacher assistant, class scores and weak topics with each learner's name replaced by a label such as "Learner 3" | To write the answer. Our agreements with them must not allow them to train their models on this data. |
| Our web hosting provider (InfinityFree) | All stored data | To run the servers and database |
| Authorities | Only what the law requires | When legally required |

Some providers are outside Nigeria. We transfer data to them only with the safeguards the NDPA requires.

## 5. Using data to improve our AI

We may want to use learners' questions, answers and chats to improve AceDrill's own AI, for example to train a model that explains Nigerian curriculum questions better.

- **This is a separate choice.** We do it only for learners whose parent or guardian said yes on the consent form, and for adult users who switch it on in their Profile.
- **Saying no changes nothing else.** Every feature works the same.
- **Before any training, we remove names, emails, schools and anything else that identifies a person.**
- You can change your mind at any time in your Profile, or through your school. Data already removed of identifying details cannot be traced back to you.

Until this is switched on for someone, their data is not used for training.

## 6. Keeping data safe

- Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
- Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Each school sees only its own learners. Learners see only their own data and chats.
- Offline copies on your device are protected by your device's lock. Sign out on shared devices.
- If a breach puts your data at risk, we tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell affected schools and users without delay.

## 7. Your rights

You, or your parent or guardian if you are under 18, can ask to:

- see the data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it, or your account;
- object to, or withdraw consent for, a use such as AI training;
- complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Ask your school, or write to {email}. We answer within 30 days.

## 8. Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, we tell schools and show the new version in the app, and ask for consent again where the law requires it.
