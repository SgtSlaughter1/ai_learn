# Product Vision

The Offline AI Learning Platform aims to bridge the digital divide in education by providing high-quality, adaptive, and AI-assisted educational resources to learners in regions with unreliable or non-existent internet access.

## Target Audience
1. **Secondary School Students**: Accessing curriculum subjects (Mathematics, English, Biology, Physics) aligned with standard academic syllabi.
2. **Teachers**: Customizing assignments, monitoring students' offline progress histories upon sync, and managing learning packages.
3. **Administrators**: Overseeing platform users, curricula, and system health.

## Core Pillars
* **High Availability**: Works anywhere, anytime, completely offline.
* **Personalized Recommendations**: Client-side adaptive grading and mastery calculation guides students' study paths.
* **Affordable AI Tutoring**: Seamlessly integrates AI explanations, question generation, and tutoring without vendor lock-in.
